Condo insurance may cover water damage, but it depends on the cause of the loss and the details of the policy. In many cases, coverage is more likely when the damage comes from a sudden and accidental event, such as a burst pipe or overflowing appliance. Coverage is often less likely when the issue developed slowly over time or resulted from lack of maintenance.

In a condo, there is another layer to consider. There may be a master policy for the building and an individual policy for the unit owner. One may apply to parts of the structure, while the other may apply to interior finishes, personal property, or loss of use. Responsibility can also change depending on where the water started and which areas were affected.

That is why the first steps matter. Stop the source if possible, document the damage, notify building management, review the master policy and your own policy, and report the claim quickly. It also helps to ask who is responsible for drywall, flooring, cabinets, and shared building systems.

Even when coverage exists, waiting too long can make the claim harder. Fast mitigation shows that reasonable steps were taken to prevent further damage.
