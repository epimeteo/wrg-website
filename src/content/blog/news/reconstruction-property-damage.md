Reconstruction for your property is critical if your property is victim of water damage or fire damage. As a property manager, board member or owner in your community, it is imperative that you have a reputable restoration company to count on. The Water Restoration Group is a licensed and insured general contractor that can not only mitigate the damages but structurally and aesthetically restore your property as well. We have 11+ years of experience, particularly servicing high-rises in all of South Florida. We are sensitive to the urgency behind high-rise buildings as an emergency can affect many people simultaneously, not just a few. We can start the project at little cost to you and bill your insurance as part of the mitigation of damages after a disaster.

## High-Rise Reconstruction

With high-rise building reconstruction, integrity, intelligence and quality work in a timely manner are our most important characteristics as licensed and insured general contractors. We know that these are indispensable when it comes to high-rise construction projects as the nature of a high-rise community is interdependent.

We have 11 years of experience restoring buildings back to normal. We believe in an open communication policy where we make it a priority to keep lines of communication open between our team and property management, residents, architects and any consultants involved. In this way, we never deviate from your vision and are able to surpass expectations. Our in-house project management team will plan, develop, analyze and evaluate every aspect that is involved in executing your project in the pre-construction phase. This allows us to anticipate and minimize future challenges during reconstruction.

We can handle even the most complex construction endeavors by implementing effective project management, selectively hiring highly qualified sub-contractors, using efficient delivery methods and committing to open communication. We’ve been serving the high-rise community of South Florida for 11+ years; focusing on excellent craftsmanship, integrity and unmatched customer service. We know exactly what you’re going through and we know exactly how to help you. The experience of a fire is hard enough. The aftermath of one doesn’t have to be. Let us make this difficult process easy for you.
