### Key Steps for Effective Hurricane Season Preparation in Florida

Below are essential actions you can still take to protect your property and ensure a faster, smoother response in the event of a storm.

#### 1. Urgently Review Contracts with Critical Vendors

If you haven’t already, now is the time to review contracts with essential service providers. Make sure:

- Agreements with roofing, electrical, debris removal, and **property restoration** companies are current and clearly outline emergency response terms.
- You have priority service clauses in place.
- You designate **WRG as your preferred restoration provider** to secure quick action after a storm.

**Pro Tip:** WRG offers priority response agreements to proactive clients—it’s not too late to enroll.

When disaster strikes, **every minute counts**. Authorized access allows vendors to begin restoration immediately without delay. Ensure that you:

- Create and sign access letters for all key vendors.
- Include clear entry instructions for cases where power or systems may be down.
- Store and share digital copies with your internal team and emergency contacts.

#### 3. Off-Site Backups and Digital Documentation

If you haven’t yet organized essential documentation, do it this week. You should:

- Back up insurance policies, service contracts, and emergency plans.
- Store documents in the cloud (Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive) with secure access.
- Share login credentials with trusted team members who will be decision-makers in a crisis.

#### 4. Staff Communication and Emergency Training

A well-designed plan is useless if your team doesn’t know how to follow it. Early in the season, take time to:

- Hold a quick meeting to review your emergency response plan.
- Assign a “restoration coordinator” to serve as the point of contact with vendors like WRG.
- Set up a dedicated communication channel (e.g., WhatsApp group, internal app, shared email thread).
