Starting January 1, 2026, Florida condominium associations managing 25 or more units will be required to establish a secure website or mobile application for hosting essential documents, in compliance with the new *Florida condominium website law*. This legislation aims to enhance transparency, improve accessibility, and ensure legal compliance across condominium associations in the state.

For property managers, this change represents a significant operational shift. Understanding the requirements and taking proactive steps to comply will be key to a seamless transition. Here’s everything you need to know and how **The Water Restoration Group** (WRG) can support you.

## Key Requirements of the 2026 Law

To meet compliance, condominium associations must fulfill the following:

#### 1. Create a Secure Website or Mobile Application

- The platform must feature a secure, password-protected section accessible only to unit owners and association employees.
- Websites or mobile applications can be managed internally or through a third-party provider, offering flexibility based on the association’s needs.

#### 2. Upload Mandatory Documents

The law requires the following documents to be hosted online:

- Recorded declarations of condominium and amendments.
- Bylaws, articles of incorporation, and rules and regulations.
- Annual budgets, financial reports, and meeting notices with agendas.
- Executed contracts and other critical records relevant to the association’s operations.

#### 3. Provide Access to Unit Owners

- Implement a system for issuing usernames and passwords to unit owners upon request, ensuring secure and controlled access to the platform.

## Why This Matters for Property Managers

Compliance with the 2026 law offers significant benefits while addressing potential challenges:

#### Streamlined Operations

A centralized digital repository reduces the time and effort needed to respond to unit owner inquiries or prepare for audits. Quick access to organized records simplifies your day-to-day operations.

#### Enhanced Transparency

Giving unit owners easy access to important documents fosters trust and strengthens communication, which are essential for maintaining a positive relationship with residents.

#### Legal Compliance

Non-compliance with the 2026 law can result in penalties, operational disruptions, and a damaged reputation for the association. Adopting these measures ensures your properties operate within legal guidelines.
