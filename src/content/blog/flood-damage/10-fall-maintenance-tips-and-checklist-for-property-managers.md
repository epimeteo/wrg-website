Today marks the first day of fall, and as a property manager, the transition to fall is substantial. Whatever tasks your property demands, it’s wise to exercise caution to avoid significant property loss and keep your tenants satisfied. With fall now in full swing and winter on the horizon, here are some tips and a checklist to help you prepare your property and make it stand out.

## Tips /Checklist

1. Maintain Landscaping
2. Maintain Recreational Facilities
3. Have Gutters Cleaned
4. Roof Inspection
5. Check Detectors
6. Repair Cracks in Parking Lot
7. Secure Any Gaps to Avoid Critters
8. Check Drainage
9. Complete Outdoor Projects
10. HVAC Maintenance
